What's real vs illustrative?

Real: existing McLaren GLB viewer, camera controls, browser AR handoff, interactive UI, local schematic animation and browser speech.

Illustrative: chassis configuration drawing, engine mechanism, physics estimates, lamp toggles and part-selection concepts. They are not validated CAD, CFD, FEA, ECU or crash-test outputs.

How the architectures differ

Battery electric vehicle (BEV)

A large traction battery supplies an inverter and electric motor. Most BEVs have no combustion engine, fuel tank or multi-speed conventional gearbox; regenerative braking can recover some kinetic energy.

What each alignment angle does

Quick reference
CamberWheel tilt viewed from the front. Influences contact patch under cornering and tyre wear.
CasterSteering-axis tilt viewed from the side. Adds self-aligning behaviour and directional stability.
Kingpin inclination (KPI)Steering-axis tilt viewed from the front. Helps create steering geometry and scrub-radius effects.
ToeWheels point inward or outward in top view. Influences straight-line stability and tyre wear.
