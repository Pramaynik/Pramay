AutoAR Studio integrated upgrade — revision 7

New in V7: dedicated Electric & Hybrid page. Includes an animated illustrative energy-flow schematic with selectable Battery EV, HEV, PHEV and ICE modes; moving energy particles, rotating motor/engine/gear/wheels; accelerator and speed sliders; battery SOC; automatic/EV-priority/charge-sustain/power strategy selector; regenerative braking action; and architecture comparisons for BEV, HEV, PHEV, MHEV and FCEV. Adds component explainers for traction battery, BMS, inverter, motor, reduction gear, on-board charger, DC-DC converter, regenerative braking, thermal management, hybrid engine, motor-generator and power-split device.

Preserves previous customizer, engine sound/voice/MCQs, suspension bay, all-parts motion board, ABS/BMS/sensor demos, and McLaren model paths. Keep both GLB files in the repository root.

Install: back up root index.html, replace with this file, commit to main, wait for Pages deployment, hard refresh. This package does not deploy itself.

Limitations: EV/hybrid flows and power numbers are educational illustrations, not a vehicle-specific simulation. Real hybrid architectures differ (series, parallel, power-split). Regeneration depends on battery limits, grip, speed and control calibration. High-voltage EV work requires qualified training and isolation. Full 3D part motion requires separate rigged meshes. Syntax checks do not establish full browser correctness.


V8 additions: prominent AR launch buttons, synthesized sound effects for intake/turbo/gearbox/brakes/suspension/exhaust, and a road-test simulator with throttle/brake/steering/grip controls, telemetry, estimated stopping distance, and a reaction-time challenge. The road and audio are illustrative browser simulations; they are not real vehicle tests or recorded McLaren sounds.
